Privacy Policy

NextLevelClips Publisher ("the app") is a private tool used only by the NextLevelClips team to publish our own short gaming clips to our own TikTok account.

What we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (display name, username, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account (video.publish).
  • The list of our own published videos and their public counts (views, likes) to measure performance (video.list).

How we store it

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI). We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not collect data from other TikTok users.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting the account in the app, or removing the app's access in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), deletes and revokes the stored token.
