Privacy Policy

NextLevelClips Publisher (also shown as "SharkyClips" on the Google sign-in screen) ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the NextLevelClips channels to publish our own short gaming clips to our own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, translations and publish times, and to read the view statistics of our own channel. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the connected channel's ID, name and thumbnail; the ability to upload and update our own videos, thumbnails and playlists; and the statistics (views, likes) of our own videos.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, username, avatar), permission to upload videos or drafts (video.publish, video.upload) and the public counts of our own videos (video.list).
  • Instagram / Facebook (Meta): the connected professional account and Page, permission to publish Reels, and basic insights of our own posts.

The app does not access, collect or store data of any other user, viewer or commenter.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to publish and schedule our own clips and to see which clips perform best.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI). Video statistics are kept locally for up to 30 days and then refreshed or deleted.
  • The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data to third parties, and does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube: you can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings – third-party access.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Meta: Settings → Apps and websites.
  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored token immediately. Any other stored data is deleted within 7 days of a request through the contact below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact us through our YouTube channel @NextLevelClips-GT.
